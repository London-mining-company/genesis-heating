## 📅 MONDAY: The "One Watt" Principle

> In thermodynamics, there is no such thing as "free" energy. But there is "recycled" energy.
>
> A standard electric water heater converts 100% of its energy into heat to warm your water. 1 watt in = 100% thermal output.
>
> The Superheat H1 does the same thing, but it performs a high-value computing task *before* releasing that heat.
>
> It’s the same watt, doing two jobs.
>
> We don't change how much energy your home uses—we just change how much of that energy you get paid for.
>
> 98% of the heat created by the internal processing is captured and transferred to your water.
>
> That’s how we turn a utility bill into a utility credit.
